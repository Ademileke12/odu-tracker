// RACE BRIEFING

THREE STEPS TO GREEN LIGHT

  1. 01 · InstallRun the installer for your platform. Everything ships inside — no Python, no Node, no admin panel.
  2. 02 · ConnectJoin the ODU's Wi-Fi / Ethernet. The app probes 192.168.0.1 and confirms it's really your ODU.
  3. 03 · DriveSign in once with your ODU admin password — the app keeps its own session and starts logging immediately.
⚠ PENALTY BOX

The ODU firmware allows only 3 wrong login attempts, then locks the web login for ~26 h. The app stores your password locally and re-auths silently — so never brute-force it, in the app or anywhere else.
