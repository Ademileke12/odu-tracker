// FIRST START

ONLINE IN THREE STEPS

  1. 01
    INSTALLRun the installer for your platform. Everything ships inside, no Python, no Node, no admin panel.
  2. 02
    CONNECTJoin the ODU's Wi-Fi / Ethernet. The app probes 192.168.0.1 and confirms it's really your ODU.
  3. 03
    DRIVESign in once with your ODU admin password, the app keeps its own session and starts logging immediately.
⚠ PENALTY BOX

The ODU firmware allows only 3 wrong login attempts, then locks the web login for ~26 h. The app stores your password locally and re-auths silently, so never brute-force it, in the app or anywhere else.
